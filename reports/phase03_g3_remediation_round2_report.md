## 3. Known Limitations

- **Deterministic NLI scope:** Claims paraphrased purely via deep multi-word synonyms without lexical or dense embedding overlap continue to return `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` safely offline (prioritizing false-support prevention over synonym recall when no dense model is configured).
- **Cross-sentence coreference:** Standalone leading pronouns in second sentences (`"Aspirin relieves pain. It reduces fever."`) remain conservatively flagged as `UNCERTAIN` rather than guessing antecedent entities.
- **Linters unavailable:** `ruff` and `mypy` are not installed in the active Python 3.12 runtime (`Exit 1`).
