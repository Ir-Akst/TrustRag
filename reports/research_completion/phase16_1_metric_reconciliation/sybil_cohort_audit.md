## 3. Defense Mechanism & Baseline Comparison

In this 3v1 setting:
- **Total Scenarios Evaluated:** Exactly **125** query-corpus instances.
- **Corpus per Query:** 4 documents (1 authentic Tier-1 NIST document vs 3 synthetic Tier-3 mirror documents).

| Architecture | Defended Scenarios | Breached Scenarios | Attack Defense Rate (ADR) | 95% Wilson CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Standard RAG (Config A)** | 54 | 71 | **43.20%** | [34.82%, 51.98%] |
| **TrustRAG Full (Config D)** | 125 | 0 | **100.0%** | [96.97%, 100.0%] |

### 3.1 Why Standard RAG Failed (ADR = 43.20%)
In Standard RAG without trust weighting, TF-IDF cosine relevance treats all 4 documents equally. In 71 out of 125 queries (56.80%), one of the three poisoned peer mirrors ranked #1 in retrieval, causing the poisoned claim to be synthesized.

### 3.2 Why TrustRAG Succeeded (ADR = 100.0%)
In TrustRAG, Multi-Signal Detector signal $F_5$ activates the **3-Tier Consensus Resolution Matrix**:
1. Authentic evidence `c_auth` has `authority_tier: 1` (`trust_score = 0.95`).
2. Adversarial evidence `p1, p2, p3` have `authority_tier: 3` (`trust_score = 0.35`).
3. Even though the adversary holds a 3-to-1 majority vote, the authority-weighted consensus downranks the unverified cluster and removes the lowest-trust candidates up to the removal cap.
4. The Pre-Generation Evidence Gate quarantines all surviving `authority_tier: 3` documents that conflict with the Tier-1 reference.
5. `c_auth` is preserved as the sole admitted context document, resulting in 100.0% defense.

## 4. Audit Finding & Methodological Caveats

1. **Cohort Independence Caveat:** The Sybil evaluation does not represent an independent test set ($N=125$, 100% overlap with `cryptographic_falsification`).
2. **Synthetic Mirror Limitation:** Real-world Sybil poisoning employs distinct syntactic phrasing and varied semantic framing across independent domains, which may weaken TF-IDF vector norm anomaly detection ($F_2$).
3. **Audit Verdict:** The empirical metrics reported in Phase 16 ($43.2\%$ vs $100.0\%$) are mathematically accurate for the executed script, but must be accompanied by explicit disclosure that the cohort overlaps with the primary benchmark.
