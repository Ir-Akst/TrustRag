## 1. Executive Summary

In accordance with Phase 15 requirements (*"Do not claim external validation unless an independent external benchmark has actually been evaluated; never manufacture external validation results"*), this report presents the live empirical evaluation of TrustRAG on two independent, externally curated research benchmarks: **RAGTruth** (ParticleMedia, ACL 2024) and **HaluEval** (RUCAIBox, EMNLP 2023).

### Crucial Empirical Finding: The Reality Gap
While TrustRAG achieved a verified **$100.0\%$ Attack Defense Rate** on internal synthetic benchmarks (where attacks followed structured numerical, unit, and antonym templates), performance on real-world external benchmarks exhibits a clear **generalization boundary**:
- **RAGTruth (Real LLM Summaries & QA):** Hallucination Defense Rate (ADR) is **$66.00\%$** (Wilson 95% CI: $[56.24\%, 74.53\%]$), Attack Success Rate (ASR) is **$34.00\%$**, and Clean False Positive Rate (FPR) is **$52.00\%$** ($F_1 = 0.6055$).
- **HaluEval (Real QA Hallucinations):** Hallucination Defense Rate (ADR) is **$89.00\%$** (Wilson 95% CI: $[81.42\%, 93.75\%]$), but Clean False Positive Rate (FPR) rises to **$98.00\%$** ($F_1 = 0.6202$) because entity-fragment answers cannot be entailed as logical propositions.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Audit Conclusion:**
> TrustRAG's $100\%$ defense score is an artifact of benchmark-targeted engineering on structured factual mutations. Against complex, open-domain natural language hallucinations generated by commercial LLMs (GPT-3.5, GPT-4, Llama-2), TrustRAG provides a baseline F1 score of $\sim 0.60\text{–}0.62$. Unrestricted, unsupervised production deployment is therefore **strictly not authorized**.

## 3. Label Mapping & Evaluation Protocol

- **Clean Ground Truth (`FACTUAL`):** The claim is fully supported by the document. A safe system must output `G3ClaimVerdict.SUPPORTED` without flagging `unsupported_flag`. Any rejection or quarantine is counted as a **False Alarm (False Positive, FP)**.
- **Adversarial Ground Truth (`HALLUCINATED` / `CONTRADICTION`):** The claim contains hallucinated or contradicted information. A safe system must output `G3ClaimVerdict.CONTRADICTED` or flag `unsupported_flag = True` (Quarantine / Abstain). Admitting the claim as `SUPPORTED` is counted as a **Security Breach (False Negative, FN)**.

## 5. Forensic Analysis of the Reality Gap

1. **Why Clean FPR is High on External Data:**
   - *The Propositional Dilemma (HaluEval):* HaluEval answers are frequently single named entities (e.g. `"Luke Hemsworth"`). An NLI model trained on natural sentence hypotheses (`premise` entails `hypothesis`) assigns `NEUTRAL` or low probability to isolated noun fragments. Because `VerificationConfig.nli_neutral_policy = "insufficient"`, neutral answers trigger `unsupported_flag = True`, causing a $98\%$ false positive rate.
   - *Complex Narrative Subordination (RAGTruth):* RAGTruth summaries contain multi-clause sentences where partial clauses are entailed but minor details are unmentioned in the passage. The NLI model flags these minor omissions as insufficient support, rejecting $52\%$ of valid summaries.
2. **Why Detection Recall is 66% on Real LLM Hallucinations:**
   - Real-world LLM hallucinations in RAGTruth are rarely simple antonym flips or numeric changes. They involve subtle relation swaps (e.g. attributing an action to the wrong government agency, or confusing temporal sequence). The symbolic regex engine ($S_5$) cannot fire on these open-domain semantic relations, leaving detection solely to the 141.9M parameter DeBERTa model, whose capacity bounds recall at $66.0\%$.
3. **Latency Profile on Real-World Document Lengths:**
   - Median latency on full-length RAGTruth news passages is **$326.71\text{ ms}$**, confirming the Phase 14 finding that full neural G3 verification on CPU cannot meet sub-50ms real-time constraints without batched inference or GPU acceleration.
