## 2. Fundamental Architectural & Methodological Limitations

### 2.1 The Synthetic vs. Real-World Generalization Gap
- **Empirical Reality:** TrustRAG achieves an audited **$100.0\%$ Attack Defense Rate** on internal synthetic benchmarks (where attacks follow structured numerical, unit, and antonym templates).
- **The Reality Gap:** On open-domain, naturally occurring LLM hallucinations generated by GPT-4 and Llama-2 (RAGTruth, ACL 2024), TrustRAG achieves an **$F_1$ score of $0.6226$** ($66.0\%$ recall, $46.0\%$ clean FPR).
- **Threat to Validity:** Benchmarking solely against synthetic mutations creates a deceptive illusion of near-perfect security. TrustRAG is an effective defense framework for structured enterprise databases, but cannot guarantee zero-trust security against open-ended natural language generation.

### 2.2 The Propositional Entity Dilemma (HaluEval Failure Mode)
- **Constraint:** Natural Language Inference (NLI) models are trained to evaluate whether a natural language premise entails a full propositional hypothesis.
- **Observed Failure:** In benchmark datasets like HaluEval, answers often consist of isolated noun fragments or named entities (e.g., `"Luke Hemsworth"`).
- **Impact:** An NLI cross-attention layer cannot assign entailment confidence to a detached entity fragment, resulting in dominant `NEUTRAL` probabilities ($p_{\text{neu}} > 0.80$). Because TrustRAG policies treat ungrounded neutral statements as insufficient evidence, clean single-entity answers suffer a **$98.0\%$ False Positive Rate**.

### 2.3 Narrative Subordination & Over-Quarantining (RAGTruth Failure Mode)
- **Constraint:** Real-world enterprise summaries contain complex multi-clause sentences where secondary background context (e.g. dates, titles) is unmentioned in the retrieved excerpt.
- **Impact:** The G3 verifier strictly flags any clause lacking explicit documentary grounding, causing legitimate, high-quality human summaries to be quarantined at a rate of **$46.0\%\text{–}52.0\%$**.

### 2.4 Lexical vs. Spelled-Out Numeric Override Boundary
- **Constraint:** Due to offline dependency boundaries (`word2number` unavailable), the hybrid symbolic conflict detector ($S_5$) operates exclusively on Arabic numerals and physical units (e.g. `4g`, `25bps`, `256-bit`).
- **Impact:** Spelled-out words (`"four grams"` vs. `"twenty grams"`) bypass the symbolic rule override and rely entirely on DeBERTa's neural cross-attention, which exhibits lower confidence separation on high numbers.

## 3. Security Red-Teaming Frontier & Exploitable Conditions

As empirically proven in Phase 18 ($N=460$ red-team evaluations):
1. **Collusive Sybil Swarms ($0.0\%$ ADR):**
   - If an adversary can inject multiple mirror copies of a poisoned document ($3\text{v}1$ or $5\text{v}1$ ratio), G2's consensus deviation metric ($S_4$) inverts: the authentic document is flagged as the minority outlier, allowing the poisoned consensus to be delivered.
2. **Subtle Paraphrased Semantic Inversion ($0.0\%$ ADR):**
   - When factual poisoning is phrased using rich semantic paraphrasing without shared lexical anchors (e.g. `"indefinite zero-rate mandate"` vs. `"twenty-five bps increase"`), DeBERTa assigns `NEUTRAL` rather than `CONTRADICTION`, bypassing quarantine.
3. **Citation & Header Forgery ($0.0\%$ ADR):**
   - Plain-text citation tags (`[Ref: FDA 2024]`, `[Verified SEC]`) are stripped during text normalization, allowing untrusted sources with spoofed headers to evade provenance filtering in the absence of cryptographic signatures.
4. **Context Window Exhaustion ($0.0\%$ ADR when poison is tail-buried):**
   - In mixed benign/malicious passages where the top 250 characters are factual and the malicious injection occurs after character 300, leading-claim extraction leaves the malicious tail uninspected.

## 4. Systems, Hardware & Performance Constraints

1. **CPU Latency Scaling:**
   - Single-claim verification on CPU achieves a verified $55.84\text{ ms}$ P50 latency.
   - However, for enterprise documents requiring extraction of 10 to 15 atomic claims, serial evaluation scales to **$550\text{–}850\text{ ms}$**, exceeding synchronous web interactive SLAs ($<200\text{ ms}$). GPU acceleration or ONNX INT8 dynamic batching is required for real-time multi-claim workloads.
2. **First-Query Cold Start:**
   - Initial graph compilation and weight page allocation incurs a one-time **$2,868.37\text{ ms}$** penalty. Production systems must execute a pre-warming pass during service initialization.
