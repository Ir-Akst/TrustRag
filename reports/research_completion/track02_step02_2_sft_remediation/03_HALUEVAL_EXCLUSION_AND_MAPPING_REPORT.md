# TrustRAG Track 2, Step 2.2: HaluEval Exclusion and Mapping Report

## 1. Upstream Generation Protocol & Inherent Semantic Limits

The **HaluEval QA Benchmark** (*Li et al., EMNLP 2023*) was constructed using an automated generation and filtering pipeline.

The generation prompt instructed ChatGPT (`gpt-3.5-turbo`):
> *"You are asked to generate a hallucinated answer that is plausible but contains factual errors or unsupported claims according to the knowledge."*

### 1.1 Inherent Semantic Conflation
1. **Factual Errors:** Directly contradict facts asserted in the context passage.
2. **Unsupported Claims:** Introduce unmentioned or fabricated entities that are completely absent from the passage.

Upstream HaluEval annotations provide only a binary signal:
- `right_answer` (ground truth answer)
- `hallucinated_answer` (non-factual answer generated by ChatGPT)

The benchmark **does not provide span annotations, conflict taxonomy, or NLI categories**.

---

## 2. Rejection of Automated 3-Way NLI Mapping for HaluEval

The Step 2.1 semantic audit evaluated a representative sample of 500 HaluEval QA records, discovering that **51.8%** of hallucinated answers represent unmentioned entities, while **48.2%** represent entity swaps or direct contradictions.

In accordance with strict conservative data engineering principles:
1. **Sample Percentages Must Not Be Extrapolated Into Per-Record Ground Truth:**  
   Statistical distributions across a 500-sample probe cannot be used to guess or hallucinate individual record-level labels for the remaining 16,500 records.
2. **Automated LLM Relabeling Is Forbidden:**  
   Using an LLM (such as GPT-4) to relabel HaluEval into `CONTRADICTION` vs `NEUTRAL` without human verification would violate the project's strict restriction against synthetic pseudo-ground-truth.
3. **No Artificial Five-Outcome Mapping:**  
   Attempting to invent a 5-outcome G3 mapping for HaluEval would inject unacceptable label noise into model training.

## 3. Remediation Policy Adopted in `sft_v2`

| Downstream Objective | Policy for HaluEval | Justification |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Binary Classification (`sft_v2/binary`)** | **INCLUDED (Remediated)** | Upstream binary annotation (`right_answer` = FACTUAL, `hallucinated_answer` = HALLUCINATED) is well-validated and authoritative. 52 leaking records quarantined. |
| **Evidence-Grounded NLI (`sft_v2/nli`)** | **STRICTLY EXCLUDED** | Conflation of contradiction and unmentioned entities makes HaluEval unsafe for 3-way NLI training. All 17,000 records quarantined. |
| **Span-Level Detection (`sft_v2/spans`)** | **STRICTLY EXCLUDED** | HaluEval QA contains zero token or character span annotations. Quarantined to prevent zero-loss masking bugs. |
| **G3 Five-Outcome (`sft_v2/g3_readiness`)** | **STRICTLY EXCLUDED** | Lacks supervision for partial support, insufficient evidence, and uncertainty. |

## 5. Future Requirements for HaluEval NLI Inclusion

Before HaluEval records can ever be included in an evidence-grounded NLI dataset:
1. **Human Taxonomy Annotation:** Annotators must classify each hallucinated answer into (a) direct contradiction or (b) unmentioned entity addition.
2. **Span Conflict Attribution:** Annotators must highlight the exact phrase in the hallucinated answer that conflicts with or exceeds the context passage.
3. **Dual Verification:** Two independent annotators must reach an inter-annotator agreement ($\kappa \ge 0.80$) on the assigned NLI label.
