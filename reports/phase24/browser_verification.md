### Trace Table Verification:
Directly below the verdict banner, the UI renders the structured **Inspection Trace** table:
- **Detection Status:** Formatted pill badge reflecting the server's decision.
- **Risk Score:** 4-decimal precision float (`1.0000` for high-severity threat, `0.4500` for confusable homoglyphs, `0.0000` for clean).
- **Responsible Layer:** `"G1 Input Guardrail (Parameterized Threat Rules & Harmful Intent Policy)"`.
- **Attack Categories:** Semicolon-separated list of triggered rules (`G1_PROMPT_INJECTION`, `G1_OBFUSCATION_DETECTED`, `G1_HARMFUL_INTENT`).
- **Reason Codes:** Audit reason codes recorded in backend logs.
- **Normalized Query:** Deobfuscated and NFKC-canonicalized text passed to downstream components.
- **Controlled Paraphrase:** Status of canonicalization (Accepted, Rejected, or Disabled).
- **Policy Explanation:** Human-readable policy rationale generated by the backend.
- **Processing Latency:** Precise round-trip milliseconds.
